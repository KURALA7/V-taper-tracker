Block and phase

Cut: the sets shown here, with singles capped at RPE 8. Build, once the cut ends: a 4th set on the priority lifts (about 100 sets a week) and singles up to RPE 9.

A deload is planned every 6th week. Take it early if two of these show up: the paused single feels a full RPE harder at the same weight two weeks running, reps drop by 2 or more at the same weight on two lifts, pec or shoulder pain, sleep or motivation clearly worse, or weight dropping faster than 1% a week.

How to run it
  • Effort. Compounds 1-2 reps in reserve. Isolation 0-1 on every set, not just the last. Bench and squat go by RPE (RPE 8 means 2 reps left).
  • Rest. Heavy bench and squat 3-4 min, other compounds 2-3 min, isolation 90-120 s, supersets 60-90 s between exercises.
  • Bench days. 5 min general warm-up and band pull-aparts, then the ramp on the first bench card. Hold a 1 s motionless pause on paused reps. Touch-and-go means a soft touch with no bounce. Moderate grip, shoulder blades back.
  • V-taper. Bench is twice a week: Monday heavy, Thursday volume. The time that freed up goes to width. Side delts get 12 sets a week from a lateral raise on every day, and lats get a third pulldown on Lower B. Getting lean does the rest for the waist.
  • Top sets (paused single and squat): log the RPE. A full point or more under target adds 2.5 kg on bench or 5 kg on squat. On target repeats, or adds if every rep moved cleanly. Half to one point over repeats. More than a point over takes 2.5 kg off per point.
  • Back-offs start at about 82% of the single (bench) or 90% of the top set (squat), corrected by how last week's hardest back-off felt. If a back-off lands a point or more off target, the next one shifts 2.5 kg per point. The tracker fills these in.
  • Everything else progresses set by set. When a set hits the top of its range at the target effort, that set alone goes up a step next time. Steps are 2.5 kg for upper-body barbell work, 5 kg for lower-body barbell work and machines, 2 kg per dumbbell and 2.5 kg on small cable work, or what your log shows, or what you set in a lift's Settings. A set that stays below the bottom of its range two sessions running drops about 7.5%.
  • Deload every 6th week, or early when two warning signs show up (see Block and phase). Keep all four days, halve the sets, use about 90% loads, stay at RPE 6 or below and skip the singles.
  • Pec. Keep bench at RPE 8 or below for the next 3-4 weeks, with no bouncing, controlled descents and an extra warm-up single. Stop at any sharp or tearing pain. A pop, bruising or loss of the front armpit fold needs urgent orthopaedic review.
  • Outside the gym. 8-12k steps a day and 2-3 easy cardio sessions of 20-40 min after lifting or on rest days. HIIT once a week at most, away from lower days. Protein about 2.0-2.4 g/kg. Aim to lose 0.5-0.8% of bodyweight a week, slowing toward 0.5% as you get leaner.
  • After the cut, switch Block and phase to Build: a 4th set on the priority lifts and singles up to RPE 9.
